Privacy Policy

Your photos, videos, and face analysis results are never sent to our servers. Your archive (collections, tags, memos, bookmarks) and face analysis results are stored only on your device. Our servers store only what is needed for sign-in and your profile.

SOFTSEED Inc. ("we") processes the personal information of users of AOMO (the "App") in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea and other applicable laws. This policy explains what information we process, why, and for how long.

1. Information we process

Account and sign-in

  • What: your Google or Apple account identifier, email address (if you use Apple's "Hide My Email," the relay address Apple creates), and sign-in method
  • Why: to identify you, keep you signed in, and manage your account
  • How long: until you delete your account

The name provided by Sign in with Apple is used only to prefill the nickname field and is not stored.

Profile

  • What: nickname, bio, D-day title and date, and the times you signed up, last edited, and last signed in
  • Why: to show your profile and check that nicknames are unique
  • How long: until you delete your account

Device and usage information

  • What: an identifier for the device the App is installed on (the identifier for vendor on iOS, the Android ID on Android), device language, App version, and the time of your first major activity
  • Why: to know which device you are currently signed in on, and to improve the service
  • How long: until you delete your account

Information collected automatically

  • Usage statistics (Google Firebase Analytics): records of how you use the App, such as screens viewed and features used, an app instance ID, and device model, OS version, and country. Used to improve the service and understand how it is used; deleted automatically after the retention period set in Firebase.
  • Error information (Google Firebase Crashlytics): where the App crashed or failed to save and its state at the time, device information, and an installation identifier. Used to fix errors; deleted 90 days after collection.

Usage statistics and error information never include your photos or videos, your archive, or face analysis results.

Email inquiries

If you contact us from the App, we receive your email address, your message, and the App version, device model, and OS version that are added to the email automatically. We use them only to reply, and delete them without delay once your inquiry is resolved.

2. Information stored only on your device

The following information is stored only on your device. We cannot see it.

  • Your archive: collections, tags, memos, bookmarks (address, title, summary, and preview image address), and recent searches
  • The identifiers of the photos and videos in your photo library, their file names, capture times, sizes, and durations, and preview images (also used by the Home Screen widget)
  • Your app lock passcode and notification settings

Original photos and videos are never copied into the App or sent to a server. If device backup is turned on (iCloud Backup or computer backups on iPhone, Google backup on Android), this information may be included in your backup, which is governed by Apple or Google and your backup settings.

3. Face information (biometric data)

People search extracts facial features from your photos and groups photos of the same person. Facial feature information is sensitive information (biometric data) under the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea, so we process it only with your separate consent, given apart from other information.

  • What: facial feature values extracted from your photos and the resulting groups of people
  • Why: to gather the people in your photos automatically, and to let you know when a new photo includes a person you have chosen. It is not used for anything else.
  • How: face detection and grouping happen entirely on your device. Photos and face information are never sent to a server or any third party, and are kept out of device backups.
  • How long and deletion: until you withdraw consent or delete the App. If you withdraw consent in Settings → Face Grouping, the analysis results are deleted immediately.
  • Your right to refuse: you do not have to consent. If you don't, only people search is unavailable; every other feature works as usual.

4. App permissions

  • Photos (required): to show the photos and videos in your library and add them to your archive. Photos are never sent to a server.
  • Notifications (optional): for reminders about unsorted photos, new photos of people you follow, and in-app messages. All notifications are local notifications scheduled on your device; no device information or push token is sent for them.

You can use the App's other features without the optional permission, and you can change permissions at any time in your device's Settings app. The App does not use location, camera, or contacts permissions.

5. Bookmarks and internet use

When you add a bookmark, the App opens the web address you entered directly on your device to read its title, summary, and preview image, and loads the preview image directly from that website. As with any web visit, the website may receive technical information such as your IP address, which is handled under that website's own policy. The App also connects to our servers to receive collage templates and announcements.

6. Sharing, processors, and international transfers

We do not share your personal information with third parties. To run the service, we entrust processing as follows, which involves a transfer of personal information outside Korea.

  • Processor and recipient: Google LLC (Google Firebase — Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Cloud Storage, Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config)
  • Purpose: account authentication, storing account and profile information, and analyzing usage statistics and errors
  • Countries: Korea, the United States, and other countries where Google operates data centers
  • When and how: sent encrypted over the internet while you use the App
  • Data and retention: the account and sign-in, profile, device and usage, and automatically collected information in section 1
  • How to refuse: delete your account and then delete the App. Features that require sign-in and stored account information will no longer be available.

Google or Apple, whichever you choose, handles account authentication during sign-in under its own privacy policy. The App does not use ad SDKs, personalized ads, or tracking across other companies' apps and websites.

7. Deletion

  • When you use Settings → Delete account, your account, profile, nickname, and device and usage information are deleted from our servers without delay.
  • Information stored only on your device is deleted when you delete the App.
  • Usage statistics and error information are not linked to your account and are deleted automatically after the periods in section 1.
  • Information we are required by law to keep is stored separately for the required period and then destroyed.

8. Your rights

You can ask at any time to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to stop its processing, and you can withdraw your consent. You can edit your profile in the App, withdraw face information consent in Settings, and delete your account with Delete account. For any other request, contact us at the address below and we will handle it without delay.

9. Children under 14

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14. If we learn that we have, we will delete it without delay.

10. Security

We take the measures needed to keep your information safe, including encryption in transit, per-account access control (security rules that let you read and write only your own data), and keeping the number of people who handle personal information to a minimum.

11. Privacy officer and contact

  • Privacy officer: SOFTSEED Inc.
  • Email: softseed@office.softseed.co.kr

For help with a privacy violation, you can also contact the Personal Information Infringement Report Center (privacy.kisa.or.kr, 118 in Korea) or the Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee (www.kopico.go.kr, 1833-6972).

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the changes and the effective date on this page and in the App's announcements at least 7 days in advance (30 days for changes that are unfavorable to you). If the App starts handling new information, we will update this page before the App update.
