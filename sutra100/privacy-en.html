Privacy Policy

Your wish and recitation history are stored on your device. Advertising, store purchases and support requests involve the services described below.

Wishes and reading history

SOFTSEED Inc. provides Sutra100 without an account. The app saves your wish, practice dates and completions, reading time and position, bookmark, language and text size on your device. It does not automatically send your wish or reading history to us or to advertising services. If you enable reminders, your device schedules them. You can control lock-screen previews in system settings.

Storage and deletion

Deleting the app removes its local data. If you use device backup or restore, manage separate backups with that service. We cannot retrieve or restore wishes or bookmarks held only on your device.

Banner and interstitial ads

Google AdMob may process IP addresses and approximate location, device and advertising identifiers, app and device information, ad impressions and clicks, performance data and errors to deliver and measure ads, diagnose problems and prevent abuse. Data use depends on platform and consent. Where available, you can change consent in the app’s ad privacy settings. Manage tracking permission on iOS or the Android advertising ID in system settings.

Ad-free subscriptions

Apple or Google handles payment. The app checks store transaction information and entitlement status when you purchase or restore ad removal. We do not receive your card number. The store manages your transaction history.

Optional features and public documents

When additional reading editions are available, your selected file is retrieved from Google Firebase Storage. Connection data may include the IP address, requested file and time. English and Korean readings are included in the app. GitHub Pages logs visitors’ IP addresses for security when they open these documents.

Support and international processing

Support opens a draft in your chosen email app. It includes your subject, message, optional reply address, app version, device, operating system and language. You can edit or cancel it before sending. Our team uses the email address and message it receives to respond and investigate problems. You do not need to include your wish or religious beliefs.

Providers and retention

Google LLC (ads and file delivery) and GitHub, Inc. (document hosting) may process the information described above in the United States or other countries where they operate when you use each feature. Your email provider’s policies also apply to the transmission and storage of your message. We promptly delete support emails once a request is resolved. If the law requires retention, we keep the required information separately for that period and then delete it.

Your rights and contact

You or your legal representative can request access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing or withdrawal of consent through the privacy contact below. We request only the information needed to verify the request. Changes and their effective date will be posted on this page.
