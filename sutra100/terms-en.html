Sutra100 is a personal practice app for writing down a wish and reflecting on it while reading the Lotus Sutra over 100 days.

Using the service

SOFTSEED Inc. provides wish writing, recitation, bookmarks, progress records and a completion ceremony without an account. The practice and ceremony support personal reflection. They do not guarantee that a wish will come true.

Reading editions

The English and Korean readings follow the 27-chapter structure of H. Kern’s 1884 English edition. Chapter order and wording may differ between editions. If a passage is unclear, send us its chapter and wording using the contact below. Additional editions, when offered, require an internet connection to download. Data charges may apply.
