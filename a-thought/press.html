Think sideways.

A Thought Press Kit

A one-minute daily writing practice for thinking sideways.
Launching November 10, 2026 on the App Store and Google Play — free, in English, Korean and Japanese.

About

Each day starts from one ordinary sentence — “Bananas are long.” You write the first thought that comes to mind, and that thought becomes the next prompt. Keep going as long as you like, then save to see the path your mind took. There are no right answers and no scores: if a thought moved even a little, that’s enough.

  • This Week’s Thoughts — days when different sentences led to the same place, the words that keep returning, the thought that traveled farthest
  • Meet Your Past Self — what you wrote the last time you met the same sentence
  • A thought tree on the home screen that grows from soil to flowers as you write
  • Thought Cloud, Brain Map and Mind Map — images made from the words you return to, saved or shared only when you choose

Writing is personal. A Thought never requires an account or sign-in, and you can always choose to keep your entries only on your device.

Why now

As AI writes more of our emails, essays and plans, many of us rarely follow a thought of our own all the way through. A Thought gives that back for one minute a day. AI only helps draft the ordinary starting sentence, which our team reviews; the thinking is entirely yours, and the app never writes or judges a thought for you.
