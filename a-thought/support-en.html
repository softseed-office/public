FAQ

Where are my thoughts stored?

Only on this device. Nothing is sent to a server, and the developer can't see it. The App only uses the internet to download new daily prompts, to open the Privacy Policy, and to send error information when something goes wrong in the App (it never includes what you write).

Can I move my records to a new device?

Yes — restoring a new device from an iCloud or computer backup brings your records along. The App has no account sync of its own.

I'm not getting the daily reminder.

Check that Settings → Daily Reminder is on in the App, and that notifications are allowed on your device (iPhone: Settings app → Notifications → A Thought / Android: Settings → Apps → A Thought → Notifications). If you've already written that day, no reminder is sent.

My images aren't saving to Photos.

On iPhone, in the Settings app → Apps → A Thought → Photos, allow Add Photos Only. On Android 10 and later no permission is needed; on Android 8–9, allow the storage permission when asked. The App only adds images; it never reads your photos.

How do I delete my records?

Delete a single flow from the More menu on its screen, or everything at once in Settings → Delete All Records. Deleted records can't be restored.

Privacy

A Thought does not collect what you write. It only collects error information to fix problems in the App. See the Privacy Policy for details.
