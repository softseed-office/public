FAQ

Where are my thoughts stored?

Only on this device. Nothing is sent to a server, and the developer can't see it. The App only uses the internet to download new daily prompts, to open the Privacy Policy, to send error information when something goes wrong in the App, and, starting with version 1.1.0, to send usage statistics such as the days you open the App and whether you saved a flow, if Send Usage Statistics is on. Neither ever includes what you write. You can turn usage statistics off anytime in Settings → Send Usage Statistics.

Can I move my records to a new device?

When you switch to the same kind of device, your device backup brings your records and settings along.

  • iPhone: when setting up your new iPhone, choose Quick Start or Restore from iCloud Backup. Restoring from a computer backup works too. Deleting and reinstalling just the App does not bring your records back.
  • Android: if your device's Google backup is on, your latest backup comes back when you set up a new device with the same Google account, or when you reinstall the App.

Backups usually run about once a day, so thoughts written after the last backup may be missing. Before switching devices, check that backup is on (iPhone: Settings → [your name] → iCloud → iCloud Backup / Android: search Settings for "Backup" — menu names vary by device).

Moving records between iPhone and Android, and syncing across several devices, aren't available yet. The App has no accounts, so your records live only on your device and in its backup.

I'm not getting the daily reminder.

Check that Settings → Daily Reminder is on in the App, and that notifications are allowed on your device (iPhone: Settings app → Notifications → A Thought / Android: Settings → Apps → A Thought → Notifications). If you've already written that day, no reminder is sent.

My images aren't saving to Photos.

On iPhone, in the Settings app → Apps → A Thought → Photos, allow Add Photos Only. On Android 10 and later no permission is needed; on Android 8–9, allow the storage permission when asked. The App only adds images; it never reads your photos.

How do I delete my records?

Delete a single flow from the More menu on its screen, or everything at once in Settings → Delete All Records. Deleted records can't be restored.

How do I cancel my Supporter subscription?

The Supporter subscription (from version 1.2.0) is canceled in the store account you subscribed with. In the App, Settings → Support and decorate → Manage Subscription opens the store's subscription page. You can also go there directly.

  • iPhone: Settings app → [your name] → Subscriptions, then choose A Thought and cancel.
  • Android: Play Store → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, then choose A Thought and cancel (menu names may vary slightly).

After you cancel, Supporter features keep working until the period you've already paid for ends. When it ends, images and videos you've saved stay as they are, and any Supporter font or background you chose goes back to the default. Writing and your records keep working as before. Deleting the App does not cancel the subscription, so be sure to cancel it in the store. For refunds, please contact the App Store or Google Play.

I switched devices or reinstalled the App, and Supporter is locked.

Your subscription lives in your store account. On a device signed in with the same Apple Account or Google account you subscribed with, the App checks it automatically when it opens. If it stays locked, tap Settings → Support and decorate → Restore Purchases in the App. You need an internet connection.

If you see "There are no purchases to restore," check in the store's subscription page that the device is signed in with the account you subscribed with and that the subscription hasn't ended. Subscriptions are separate for each store, so one bought on iPhone can't be used on Android, or the other way around. The App has no accounts of its own; the store account is all it checks.

Privacy

A Thought does not collect what you write. It only collects error information to fix problems in the App and, starting with version 1.1.0, usage statistics to help improve it if Send Usage Statistics is on. See the Privacy Policy for details.
