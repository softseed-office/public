A Thought

Privacy Policy

A Thought does not collect the thoughts you write. Your thoughts and settings stay on your device. If the App crashes or fails to save your records, it sends error information to Google Firebase Crashlytics so we can fix the problem. Starting with version 1.1.0, if Send Usage Statistics is on, the App also sends usage statistics, such as the days you open it and whether you saved a flow, to Google Analytics. You can turn this off anytime in Settings. Also starting with version 1.1.0, messages you send from Settings → Contact Us are delivered to our work messaging (Slack) channel.

1. Data we collect

A Thought (the "App"), provided by softseed ("we"), has no accounts or sign-in, and does not collect the thoughts you write or information such as your name, email, or location. Messages you choose to send us are described in section 8.

To fix problems in the App, it automatically collects the following error information.

  • What: where the App crashed (stack trace) and its state at the time, the type of error when saving, loading, or deleting records fails, device information (device model, OS version, memory and storage status, etc.), and a Crashlytics installation ID (a random value created for each installation of the App)
  • Why: to find and fix the causes of errors, and to know how many users are affected
  • How long: 90 days from collection, then deleted

Error information never includes the thoughts you write, and we cannot tell who you are from it.

Starting with version 1.1.0, to help us improve the App, it also collects the following usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase), only while Settings → Send Usage Statistics is on.

  • What: the day you first opened the App, the days you return and how long you use it (collected automatically by Google Analytics), whether you saved a thought flow and whether it was the first one on this device, whether you allowed notifications and the time of day you chose (morning or evening), an app instance ID (a random value created for each installation of the App), device information (device model, OS version, App version, language), and approximate location estimated from your IP address (country and city level)
  • What we never collect: the sentences and thoughts you write, information such as your name or email, or advertising identifiers (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android). The App does not automatically record which screens you open.
  • Why: to learn whether people come back to the App and where they stop, so we can improve it
  • Default: in the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland, this starts off, and nothing is sent until you turn it on. Elsewhere, it starts on. You can turn it off anytime in Settings, and nothing is sent from then on.
  • How long: individual records are deleted automatically under our Google Analytics retention setting (14 months); after that, only aggregate statistics that cannot identify anyone remain.

Usage statistics never include the thoughts you write either, and we cannot tell who you are from them.

2. Data stored only on your device

The following data is created as you use the App and is stored only on your device. We cannot see it.

  • The thoughts you write and when you wrote them
  • Whether the daily reminder is on, and its time
  • Whether you have seen the welcome screen
  • Your Send Usage Statistics setting, and whether you have answered the usage statistics card on Home

If device backup is turned on (iCloud Backup or computer backups on iPhone, Google backup on Android), this data may be included in your backup, which is governed by Apple or Google and your backup settings.

3. Internet use

To get new daily prompts, the App downloads a single public file from the internet, and when you open this Privacy Policy from Settings, it loads this page (the daily prompts file on Google Firebase Hosting, this page on GitHub Pages). The App sends no information when doing so, and never transmits your records or device identifiers. Error information and usage statistics are sent only to Google Firebase (Crashlytics and Google Analytics), as described in section 1. Support messages are sent only when you send one, as described in section 8. As with any internet connection, the servers hosting these files may receive technical information such as your IP address, but we do not collect or use it. Every feature of the App except viewing this Privacy Policy and sending a support message works without an internet connection.

4. Notifications

The daily reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device. No device information or push token is sent anywhere. On Android, the App asks for notification permission and reschedules reminders on your device after it restarts. You can turn reminders off at any time in the App's Settings tab or in your device's Settings app.

5. Photos

When you export a thought flow, Thought Cloud, or Brain Map as an image, the App adds the image to your device's photos only if you choose to save it. On iPhone, the App asks only for permission to add photos. On Android 10 and later, it saves to an "A Thought" photo folder without any permission; on Android 8–9, it asks for storage write permission only when you save. The App never reads your photos or sends them anywhere. If you choose to share, the image goes only to the app you pick.

6. Sharing, processors, international transfers, and tracking

We do not share your information with third parties. The error information and usage statistics in section 1 and the in-app support messages in section 8 are processed on our behalf as follows, which involves a transfer outside your country.

  • Processor and recipient: Google LLC (Firebase Crashlytics, Google Analytics for Firebase, Cloud Functions for Firebase, Firebase App Check)
  • Purpose: storing and analyzing App error information, App usage statistics, and delivering and verifying in-app support messages
  • Countries: the United States and other countries where Google operates data centers (the server that delivers support messages is in the Seoul, South Korea region)
  • When and how: error information the next time you open the App after an error, usage statistics in batches from time to time while you use the App, and support messages when you send them, sent encrypted over the internet
  • Data, purpose, and retention: as in sections 1 and 8 (support messages are only passed through, not stored on the server)
  • How to opt out: turn off Settings → Send Usage Statistics to stop usage statistics. The App has no setting to turn off error reporting; deleting the App stops it. Instead of sending a support message in the App, you can email us at the address in section 10.
  • Processor and recipient: Slack Technologies, LLC (Slack)
  • Purpose: delivering and keeping in-app support messages
  • Countries: the United States and other countries where Slack operates data centers
  • When and how: when you send a support message, sent encrypted over the internet
  • Data, purpose, and retention: as for in-app support messages in section 8
  • How to opt out: email us at the address in section 10 instead of sending a message in the App.

The App uses no advertising or tracking, and error information and usage statistics are never used for advertising or tracking. Consent for ad storage and ad personalization is always set to denied.

7. Deleting your data

  • Go to Settings → Delete All Records in the App to erase every thought.
  • You can also delete individual flows or thoughts from your records.
  • Deleting the App removes all data it stored on your device.
  • Error information is deleted automatically 90 days after collection. Because it is not linked to who you are, we cannot find and delete a specific user's error information.
  • Turning off usage statistics in Settings stops them from being sent, and records already sent are deleted automatically after 14 months. As with error information, we cannot find and delete a specific user's records.
  • Support messages sent from the App are deleted from the Slack channel once your inquiry is resolved. To have one deleted sooner, email us at the address in section 10.

8. Contacting us

Support messages in the App (from version 1.1.0): only when you tap Send in Settings → Contact Us, the App sends the following.

  • Data: message type, your message, the email address for our reply (if you enter one), App version, OS version, device model, and App language
  • Purpose: reading and answering your message, and fixing problems
  • How: the message passes through our server (Cloud Functions for Firebase) straight to our work messaging (Slack) channel and is not stored in the App or on the server. Only the items above are sent to Slack; no device identifier or other information that could identify you is passed on.
  • Device check: to confirm a message comes from a genuine install of the App, the App uses Google Firebase App Check when you send it. Apple App Attest (iPhone) or Google Play Integrity (Android) checks the integrity of the device and App, and the App sends only a verification token. It is used only to prevent abuse and is not stored.
  • Retention: deleted once your inquiry is resolved, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

By email: If you email us, we receive your email address and message in order to reply. We use them only to respond, and delete them once your inquiry is resolved, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.

9. Children

The App does not collect any information from anyone, of any age, other than the error information in section 1, usage statistics if turned on, and support messages you send as described in section 8.
