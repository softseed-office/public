Playing Top

Privacy Policy

Your face photos and rankings never leave this device. Playing Top has no sign-up and does not ask for your name, email or similar details. Google, which shows the ads, and Google Firebase, which delivers the app's settings and produces usage statistics, may process device information.

SOFTSEED Inc. ("we") processes the personal information of users of Playing Top ("the App") in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea and other applicable laws. This policy explains what information is processed, why, and for how long.

1. Face photos

  • The feature that puts a face on top of the spinning top only uses photos you pick yourself from your device. The App uses the system photo picker, so it cannot see your whole photo library and does not ask for photo permission.
  • The photo you pick is cropped and saved only on your device (one per slot, P1 to P5 in Battle). It is never sent to us or to any other server.
  • Picking another photo for the same slot replaces the previous one, and deleting the App removes them all.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

  • The App is free and shows Google AdMob ads (banner and interstitial).
  • Information processed: advertising identifier (iOS IDFA, Android advertising ID), IP address, device, OS and app information, records of ads viewed or tapped
  • Purpose: showing ads, measuring ad performance, preventing invalid clicks, managing consent
  • Ad consent: in regions that require consent, such as the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your consent before any ads are loaded. You can change it at any time in the App under Settings → Ad privacy options.
  • iOS tracking permission: the App asks for App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission before using the advertising identifier for personalized ads. If you decline, the identifier is not used and you see non-personalized ads; the number of ads stays the same. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android advertising ID: you can delete or reset it in your device settings under Privacy (or Google) → Ads.
  • With a Remove Ads subscription, the App does not load ads.
  • How Google processes data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, Google Privacy Policy

3. App settings and usage statistics (Google Firebase)

  • The App uses Google Firebase Remote Config to receive app settings such as the interstitial ad interval and the version numbers used for new-version notices.
    • Information processed: Firebase installation ID (a new value created each time the App is installed), IP address, app version, device and OS information
    • Purpose: delivering app settings. It is not used to identify you or for ads.
  • Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase): we produce usage statistics to learn which modes are used and how, so we can improve the App.
    • Information processed: app usage (game start and end, the mode chosen (In place, Arena, Battle), the number of players in Battle, how long the top spun), taps on Remove ads and Restore purchases and their result (purchased, cancelled, pending, failed), when a new-version notice was shown and tapped, whether you have a Remove Ads subscription, app instance ID (a new value created each time the App is installed), approximate location estimated from the IP address (country and city level), device, OS and app information. The advertising identifier may be processed with them only when your ad consent and your device's advertising identifier settings allow it.
    • Face photos, top designs, names entered in the ranking, email addresses and similar details are never sent.
    • In regions that require ad consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and others), statistics are stored only if you consent to storage for analytics.
    • Retention: individual records are deleted automatically under the Google Analytics retention setting (up to 14 months). After that, only aggregate statistics that cannot identify anyone remain.
  • See also: Privacy and Security in Firebase

4. In-app subscription (Remove Ads)

  • Purchases and payments for the Remove Ads subscription are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never receive card numbers or other payment details.
  • The App only checks with the store whether you are subscribed and saves that on your device. It checks again when the App starts and when you use Restore purchases.

5. Information stored on your device

The following is stored only on your device and we cannot see it. It is deleted when you delete the App.

  • Face photos (section 1), top designs (patterns and colors), the mode and number of players you chose
  • Ranking: the top 10 records (spin times) with the names you entered (up to 10 characters), and your best record
  • The game count used to space interstitial ads, and the version and time you chose "Later" on a new-version notice
  • Whether you have a Remove Ads subscription, and your ad consent status

6. App permissions

  • Tracking (iOS, optional): used for the advertising identifier in personalized ads.

Every feature works without it, and you can change it at any time in your device's Settings app. The App does not use location, camera, microphone or contacts permissions, and only receives the photos you pick, as described in section 1.

7. Sharing, processors and international transfer

We do not sell your personal information or provide it to third parties. To show ads and run the App, we entrust processing as follows, and information is transferred abroad as a result.

  • Processor (recipient): Google LLC (Google AdMob, User Messaging Platform, Firebase Remote Config, Google Analytics for Firebase, Cloud Functions, App Check)
  • Work entrusted: showing and measuring ads, managing ad consent, delivering app settings, app usage statistics, forwarding support inquiries and verifying app requests
  • Countries: the United States and other countries where Google operates data centers
  • When and how: sent encrypted over the internet while you use the App
  • Items and retention: the information in sections 2 and 3 and the support inquiries in section 9. Kept for the period set by Google's retention policies; support inquiries are only forwarded and are not stored on Google's servers.
  • How to refuse: you can decline ad consent or turn off iOS tracking or the Android advertising ID. If you do not want your information transferred abroad, you can delete the App.
  • Processor (recipient): Slack Technologies, LLC (Slack)
  • Work entrusted: forwarding and keeping support inquiries
  • Countries: the United States and other countries where Slack operates data centers
  • When and how: sent encrypted over the internet when you send a support inquiry
  • Items and retention: the same as the support inquiries in section 9
  • How to refuse: instead of Contact us in the App, you can email us at the address in section 11.

In-app subscriptions are handled by Apple or Google, whichever you use, under that company's privacy policy.

8. Deletion

  • Information stored on your device is deleted when you remove it in the App or delete the App.
  • Information processed by Google is deleted under Google's retention policies.
  • Support inquiries are deleted from the Slack channel without delay once they have been answered and handled.
  • Email inquiries are deleted without delay once they have been answered and handled. If a law requires us to keep them, we keep them separately for that period and then destroy them.

9. Support inquiries and email

When you send an inquiry from Settings → Contact us in the App, we process the following.

  • Items: subject, message, the email address for our reply (if you enter one), app name, version and build number, platform and OS version, device model, app language
  • Purpose: reading and answering inquiries, fixing problems
  • How it is handled: our server (Google Cloud Functions) receives it and, without storing it, forwards it straight to a channel in our team's work messenger (Slack). Several of our apps share that channel, so the app name is sent with it. No advertising identifier or other information that could identify you is sent with it.
  • Verifying app requests: Google Firebase App Check confirms that the inquiry came from a genuine copy of Playing Top. For this, Apple App Attest on iOS and Google Play Integrity on Android check the integrity of the device and the App. This is used only to prevent abuse.
  • Retention: deleted without delay once answered and handled.

If you email the address in section 11, we receive your email address and the message. We use them only to reply and delete them without delay once handled.

10. Your rights and children

You can ask at any time to access, correct, delete or stop the processing of your personal information, and you can withdraw consent. You can change ad consent, tracking permission, the advertising ID and face photos yourself in the App and in your device settings. For anything else, contact us at the address below and we will handle it without delay.

The App is not directed at children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we post the changes and the effective date on this page 7 days before they take effect (30 days for changes that are unfavorable to you). If the App starts handling new information, we update this page before the App update.
