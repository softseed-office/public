Geulk (긁)

Privacy Policy

Your recordings never leave this device. Geulk has no sign-up and does not ask for your name, email or similar details. Google, which shows the ads, and Google Firebase, which delivers the app's settings, may process device information.

SOFTSEED Inc. ("we") processes the personal information of users of Geulk ("the App") in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea and other applicable laws. This policy explains what information is processed, why, and for how long.

1. Microphone and recordings

  • The Bounce Back (반사), Copycat (따라하기) and My Sounds (내 소리) features record sound with the microphone. The App asks for microphone permission the first time you use them. If you decline, only the recording features are unavailable.
  • Recordings are played, altered and saved only on your device. They are never sent to us or to any other server.
  • Bounce Back and Copycat recordings are not kept. A temporary file is created on the device only when you share one.
  • Sounds saved in My Sounds are deleted when you delete them in the App or uninstall the App.
  • When you tap Share, the selected sound file is passed to the app you choose (such as a messenger). From then on, that app's policy applies.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

  • The App is free and shows Google AdMob ads (banner and interstitial ads).
  • Information processed: advertising identifier (iOS IDFA, Android advertising ID), IP address, device, OS and app information, and records of ads viewed or tapped
  • Purpose: showing ads, measuring ad performance, preventing invalid clicks, and managing consent
  • Ad consent: In regions where consent is required, such as the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the App asks for consent through Google's consent form before loading ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → 광고 동의 다시 설정 (Ad consent) in the App.
  • iOS tracking permission: Before using the advertising identifier for personalized ads, the App asks for App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission. If you do not allow it, the identifier is not used and you see non-personalized ads. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android advertising ID: You can delete or reset it in your device settings → Privacy (or Google) → Ads.
  • If you buy Remove Ads, the App no longer loads ads.
  • How Google uses data: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services, Google Privacy Policy

3. App settings and content (Google Firebase)

  • The App uses Google Firebase Remote Config to receive settings such as the ad interval and voice settings, and downloads menus and sound effects from Cloud Storage.
  • Information processed: Firebase installation ID (a value created anew each time the App is installed), IP address, app version, and device and OS information
  • Purpose: delivering app settings and content. It is not used to identify you or for ads.
  • See also: Privacy and Security in Firebase

4. In-app purchase (Remove Ads)

  • The Remove Ads purchase and payment are handled by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not receive card numbers or other payment details.
  • The App only checks with the store whether you own the purchase, saves that on the device, and checks again when you tap Restore Purchase.

5. Information stored on your device

The following is stored only on your device. We cannot see it, and it is deleted when you uninstall the App.

  • Recordings and their names saved in My Sounds
  • How many times you have used the App, the count used to space interstitial ads, and the voice you chose
  • Whether you bought Remove Ads, and your ad consent status

6. App permissions

  • Microphone (optional): records sound to play and alter it on the device. Recordings are not sent to any server.
  • Tracking (iOS, optional): uses the advertising identifier for personalized ads.

You can use the other features without these permissions and change them at any time in your device settings. The App does not use location, camera, photos or contacts permissions.

7. Sharing, processors and international transfer

We do not sell your personal information or provide it to third parties. We use the following processor for ads and app operation, which involves transferring information outside Korea.

  • Processor (recipient): Google LLC (Google AdMob, User Messaging Platform, Firebase Remote Config, Cloud Storage)
  • Tasks: showing and measuring ads, managing ad consent, delivering app settings and content
  • Countries: the United States and other countries where Google operates data centers
  • When and how: sent encrypted over the internet while you use the App
  • Items and retention: the information in sections 2 and 3, kept for the period set by Google's retention policies
  • How to refuse: you can decline ad consent or turn off iOS tracking or the Android advertising ID. If you do not want any transfer, uninstall the App.

In-app purchases are handled by Apple or Google, under their own privacy policies.

8. Deletion

  • Information stored on your device is deleted when you delete it in the App or uninstall the App.
  • Information processed by Google is deleted under Google's retention policies.
  • Email inquiries are deleted without delay once answered. Where the law requires us to keep them, we store them separately for that period and then delete them.

9. Email inquiries

If you email the contact in section 11, we receive your email address and the message. We use them only to reply and delete them without delay once your inquiry is resolved.

10. Your rights and children

You may at any time ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, ask us to stop processing it, or withdraw consent. You can change ad consent, tracking, the advertising ID and microphone permission yourself in the App and in your device settings. For anything else, contact us below and we will handle it without delay.

The App is not directed at children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14.

11. Privacy officer and contact

  • Privacy officer: SOFTSEED Inc.
  • Email: softseed@office.softseed.co.kr

For help with a privacy violation, you can also contact the Personal Information Infringement Report Center (privacy.kisa.or.kr, 118 in Korea) or the Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee (www.kopico.go.kr, 1833-6972).

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the changes and the effective date on this page at least 7 days in advance (30 days for changes that are unfavorable to you). If the App starts handling new information, we will update this page before the App update.
